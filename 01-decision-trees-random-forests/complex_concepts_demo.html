💡 Why does this work mathematically?

Each doctor is wrong in a different way. Doctor 1 missed age as a factor. Doctor 2 underweighted chest pain. Doctor 3 missed vessels. But when they VOTE — the individual errors cancel each other out. The correct answer survives the majority vote.

This is the mathematical foundation of Random Forest. 100 diverse trees, each trained on different data and different features, vote together. Their errors are uncorrelated — so they cancel. This is why ensemble methods almost always beat any single model.
